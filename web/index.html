1 · Where your video comes from

Use the built-in camera studio below, or point any encoder (OBS, Streamlabs, ffmpeg) at this ingest URL. One ingest, many destinations.

rtmp://localhost:1935/live/studio

2 · Camera studio

Optional. Use your camera without OBS. Leave it off if you are already sending video from an encoder.

Publishing sends the selected camera to the ingest with a local encoder, so no separate app is needed. Start the stream first.

3 · Stream destinations

Each destination is an RTMP URL plus a stream key from that platform. Keys are encrypted on this machine and never sent back to the browser.
